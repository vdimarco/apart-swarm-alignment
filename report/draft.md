## Abstract (target 150–250 words)

Swarm alignment usually treats consensus among many agents as a safety feature. We test it directly. In an observational analysis of ~1,800 social-deduction game-days played by frontier models (pre-existing data, disclosed), eight players count as fewer than two independent forecasters within two days of deliberation, and the 75–90% agreement band executes the innocent more often than the guilty. We then replicate and extend the core phenomena in a controlled panel experiment with known ground truth: panels of six models from six distinct families agree at ρ≈0.88 before any exchange, and two rounds of discussion collapse them to an effective population of 1.01 — even when first estimates are publicly committed, and even when one panelist is structurally assigned to dissent (the dissenter itself reaches ρ=0.995 with the majority). Same-weights copies agree more than cross-family pairs by +0.082 (CI 0.074–0.089), closely replicating the +0.078 observational estimate — except at the certainty ceiling, where the premium vanishes (+0.0004). Agreement-based collusion detection is shown unworkable: it flags 100% of honest copies and 66–97% of honest strangers across thresholds. One structure works where behavioral remedies failed: partitioning evidence across panel members decorrelates priors (ρ 0.88→0.55) [final jigsaw verdict pending]. [150–250 final on completion]

## 1. Introduction

Hammond et al. (2025) and the METR Hugging Face incident (2026) motivate swarm-level safety: failures emerge between agents, not within one. Prior work documents correlated errors in LLM forecaster swarms (Oracle's Fingerprint, 2026: ρ≈0.74–0.82) and prediction-market monoculture (2026: ρ=0.70 → N_eff=1.38/10), and the October collusion sprint surfaced the "monoculture looks like collusion" trap. Missing: (a) controlled causal evidence that *deliberation itself* destroys independence, (b) a tested remedy, (c) a discriminative account separating architectural monoculture from agentic collusion.

**Contributions.**
1. Controlled replication of belief herding with per-scenario ground truth, across 6 open model families (~6.4k logged calls).
2. Remedy arc: private commitment and designated dissent **fail**; evidence partitioning decorrelates priors [and, pending, survives deliberation to degree X].
3. A three-layer discriminator (pre-talk gap, ceiling saturation, mechanism-proofness) separating architectural monoculture from agentic collusion.
4. Quantified unworkability of agreement-based collusion detection (FPR 66–97% on honest strangers).

## 2. Observational study: "Why Towns Hang Their Own" (pre-existing; weekend work = framing only)

From ~1,800 LLM social-deduction game-days (Claude/GPT/mixed teams):
- 8 players → <2 effective forecasters by day 2, →≈1 by day 5.
- Executions in the 75–90% agreement band are wrong 62% of the time; ≥90% band wrong 1%.
- Evil players nominate wrong targets 4× more, but good players make ~70% of wrong-execution nominations (herding, not just deception).
- Same-model pairs agree more than mixed pairs (+0.078, p<10⁻³, 8,275 pairs, controlling for private whispering and role).
- Negative results: extremizing worsens Brier (0.980 vs 0.908 blind); no aggregation scheme beats plain mean; best individual unidentifiable ex ante.

## 3. Controlled experiment

**Design.** Village case-file bank generated by a deterministic grammar; every non-placebo item machine-checked to have exactly one culprit (plus derived hard bank: 8 suspects, decoy ruled out only by a negative sighting). Panels of 6 agents (arms: 6-family mixed M6; same-model copies D; jigsaw M6J; devil's-advocate M6DA) judge each case in three conditions: C1 one-shot, C2 free two-round deliberation, C3 publicly committed first read + same deliberation. Strict-JSON probability elicitation, no LLM judging anywhere, append-only call logs, pre-registered hypotheses (commit b01f872, frozen before first call).

**Controls.** Derived placebo bank (rotated, inert clues) for the honest-agreement baseline; uniform-guess Brier as the non-LLM reference; matched full-information baselines for every intervention.

## 4. Results

**R1 — Deliberation collapses panels (H1, replicated).** v1 bank: initial ρ 0.875/N_eff 1.11 → final 0.982–0.992/N_eff ≈1.01 in both discourse conditions. Hard bank: initial 0.862 → final 0.995. Direction matches the observational day-by-day collapse.

**R2 — The same-weights premium is real but conditional (H3/H5).** Same-model copies vs cross-family: v1 gap **+0.082** (CI 0.074–0.089), replicating +0.078 observationally. Hard bank gap **+0.0004** (CI 0.0002–0.0007): the premium lives in tail beliefs and saturates at the certainty ceiling. Monoculture premium ≠ persistent property; it is uncertainty-conditional.

**R3 — The danger band does not manufacture by difficulty (H2, honest null).** Two difficulty tiers (incl. negative-clue-only rule-outs) yield 100% panel accuracy; the 75–90% band never populates. The observational error band rides on *unverifiable social evidence* (bluffing, role claims), not on deduction difficulty.

**R4 — Behavioral remedies fail (H4, H4').** Committed first reads collapse identically (0.867→0.992). A designated devil's advocate collapses too (0.891→0.990), and the dissenter's final beliefs match the majority in 30/30 panels (ρ=0.995): dissent is absorbed, not merely outvoted.

**R5 — Structural remedy: evidence jigsaw decorrelates priors.** With each clue shown to 3 of 6 panelists, initial agreement drops ρ 0.875→**0.55**. [Jigsaw final-stage verdict: PENDING tonight's run.]

**R6 — Agreement-based collusion detection is unworkable (Track 4).** Across thresholds ρ∈{0.5…0.99}, naive flagging hits 100% of honest copies and 66–97% of honest strangers; calibrated thresholds (μ_cross+2σ=1.27) flag nobody. Pair-level separation is absent; only group-level premium (R2) is measurable.

## 5. Discussion (the four questions)

1. **Can a remedy hold under deception?** Behavioral ones fail even without deceivers (R4); only evidence-structure changes (R5) and locked-vote verdicts alter the prior-collapse mechanism the towns show deception merely accelerates.
2. **Repeatable "clearly right, aligned-looking, daylight discarded" configuration:** R1/R6 — consensus can be correct while structurally a single stochastic process.
3. **Collusion vs. narrow architecture:** three-layer discriminator — pre-talk gap (R2), ceiling saturation (R2 hard), mechanism-proofness (R4).
4. **Structural protections:** evidence partitioning (R5) is the only measured candidate; commitment/dissent/personas/aggregation refuted by R4 and observational stark negatives.
